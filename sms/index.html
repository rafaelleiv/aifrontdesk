SMS Program Terms and Opt-In Disclosure

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This page describes the SMS messaging program operated by Rafael Leyva (sole proprietor), referred to here as “we” or “us”. We provide an AI receptionist service that answers calls and text messages on behalf of small businesses, such as clinics and service providers.

How you opt in

Consent is customer-initiated. You opt in by sending a text message to a business phone number that is served by our AI receptionist. We do not add anyone to this program from a purchased list, and we do not send marketing or promotional messages.

The business phone number is published by the business itself — for example on its own website, its appointment confirmations, its printed materials, or its storefront. By sending a text message to that number, you are asking the business a question and consenting to receive a reply by text at the number you texted from.

The first reply you receive identifies the business and includes opt-out instructions, for example:

“[Business Name]: thanks for your message — here is the information you asked for. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.”

What messages you will receive

  • Replies to the questions you send us (hours, services, availability, directions).
  • Appointment confirmations, changes and cancellations that you request.
  • Appointment reminders for appointments you have booked.

We do not send marketing, promotional or advertising messages through this program, and we do not share your phone number with third parties for their own marketing.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on your own activity. Most conversations are a handful of messages. You will typically receive one reply per message you send, plus up to two reminders for each appointment you book.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. We do not charge you for these messages; your mobile carrier’s standard rates apply.

How to stop receiving messages

Reply STOP to any message to opt out. You will receive one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages after that. You can opt back in at any time by texting the business again.

Reply HELP at any time for help, or contact us at the address below.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is not guaranteed and depends on your carrier and your device.

Privacy and terms

Your information is handled as described in our Privacy Policy, and use of the service is subject to our Terms of Service.
