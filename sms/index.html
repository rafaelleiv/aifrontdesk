SMS Program Terms and Opt-In Disclosure

Last updated: September 4, 2026

This page describes the SMS messaging program operated by Rafael Leyva (sole proprietor), referred to here as “we” or “us”. We provide an AI receptionist service that answers calls and text messages on behalf of small businesses, such as clinics and service providers.

How you opt in

Consent is customer-initiated: you text us first. There is exactly one way to opt in to this program, and this is it.

Text +1 (708) 584-4334

Send a text message to +1 (708) 584-4334 from your own phone — for example “What are your hours?” or “Can I move my appointment?”. By texting that number you are asking us a question and consenting to receive text replies from AI FrontDesk at the number you texted from.

Message frequency varies with your own activity: typically one reply per message you send, plus up to two reminders for each appointment you book. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

That number is answered by our AI receptionist and is published here, and by the business it serves on its own materials — its website, its appointment confirmations, its printed materials or its storefront.

We use no other opt-in path. There is no web form, no pre-checked box, no keyword campaign, no purchased or rented list, and no third-party lead generation. Nobody is added to this program by us: the first message always comes from you.

The first reply you receive identifies the business and includes opt-out instructions, for example:

“AI FrontDesk: thanks for your message — here is the information you asked for. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.”

What messages you will receive

  • Replies to the questions you send us (hours, services, availability, directions).
  • Appointment confirmations, changes and cancellations that you request.
  • Appointment reminders for appointments you have booked.

We do not send marketing, promotional or advertising messages through this program. We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not engage in affiliate marketing or third-party lead generation.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on your own activity. Most conversations are a handful of messages. You will typically receive one reply per message you send, plus up to two reminders for each appointment you book.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. We do not charge you for these messages; your mobile carrier’s standard rates apply.

How to stop receiving messages

Reply STOP to any message to opt out. You will receive one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages after that. You can opt back in at any time by texting +1 (708) 584-4334 again.

Reply HELP at any time for help, or contact us at the address below.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is not guaranteed and depends on your carrier and your device.

Privacy and terms

Your information is handled as described in our Privacy Policy, and use of the service is subject to our Terms of Service.
