AI FrontDesk

An AI receptionist that answers phone calls and text messages on behalf of small businesses — clinics, salons and service providers — so a customer always gets an answer.

What it does

Answers questions

Opening hours, services, location and availability, by phone or text.

Books appointments

Creates, changes and cancels appointments, and confirms them by text.

Sends reminders

Reminds customers about appointments they have booked.

Text messaging

Customers reach a business by texting its published phone number, and receive replies at the number they texted from. We send no marketing messages. Message and data rates may apply; reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Full details are on the SMS Program page.
