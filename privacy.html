Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This policy explains what Rafael Leyva (sole proprietor) collects when you call or text a business phone number served by our AI receptionist, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your phone number, and the business number you contacted.
  • The content of the messages you send and the replies we send you.
  • Call metadata (time, duration, outcome) and, where a call is transcribed, its transcript.
  • Appointment details you provide, such as name, date and requested service.

Why we collect it

To answer your question, to book, change or cancel your appointment, to send you reminders you asked for, and to keep a record of the conversation for the business you contacted.

Who we share it with

We use service providers to operate the service. They process data on our behalf:

  • Twilio — telephone and SMS delivery.
  • OpenAI — generating replies and summarising conversations.
  • Supabase — database and application hosting.
  • Stripe — payment processing, for business customers only.

The business you contacted can see your conversation with it. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your phone number with third parties for their marketing. Mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties for their own purposes.

How long we keep it

We keep conversation and appointment records for as long as the business you contacted maintains an account with us, and delete or anonymise them within a reasonable period after that account closes.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text message to stop receiving messages.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your information by writing to the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

Security

Data is stored encrypted in transit and at rest by our hosting provider, and access is restricted to the business you contacted and to the people who operate the service.

Children

The service is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. See also our SMS Program Terms and Terms of Service.
